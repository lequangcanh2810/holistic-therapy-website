# Typography

The visual language relies on a single font family with strong contrast between bold headings and readable body text.

## Font family

- Primary font: `Roboto Condensed`, sans-serif.
- Imported from Google Fonts in `src/index.css`.
- The app uses this family globally for headings, body copy, buttons, and labels.

## Font weights

- `400` — regular body copy.
- `500` / `600` — medium / strong copy and subtitle text.
- `700` — bold headings, labels, and button copy.
- `900` — heavy emphasis for hero headlines and strong accents.

## Text scales

### Headings

- `32px` — Hero headline and primary CTA heading text.
- `28px` — Section titles and important heading blocks.
- `21px` — Subheadings, accordion titles, card prices, and bold label text.

### Body text

- `18px` — Default paragraph text and list text.
- `16px` — Footer and caption text.

## Line height patterns

- `leading-normal` or explicit `leading-[25px]` for body paragraphs.
- `leading-[35px]` for section headings.
- `leading-[25px]` for smaller blocks of text to keep content dense but readable.

## Typography principles

- Use bold weights for hierarchy rather than extra font families.
- Keep body copy at a comfortable 18px size with good line height.
- Headings use strong uppercase-like emphasis and dark teal or gold accent color.
- Maintain a single type family to preserve a calm, consistent brand voice.
